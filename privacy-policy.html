Privacy Policy

App: RepForGex
Developer: Roman Shvietsov
Last updated: July 3, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide and improve our services:

  • Google account information – name, email address, and profile photo, collected when you sign in via Google.
  • Body metrics – weight, height, age, and gender, provided by you to calculate nutrition goals.
  • Nutrition and meal data – food entries, calorie counts, and macronutrients you log in the app.
  • Language preference – your selected interface language.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your identity and maintain your account.
  • To calculate personalized nutrition goals.
  • To display your meal history and nutrition statistics.
  • To provide AI-powered nutrition analysis features.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with:

  • Google – for authentication via Google Sign-In.
  • Google Play Billing – for processing in-app purchases.
  • AI analysis providers – anonymized food descriptions for nutrition estimates.

4. Data Security

All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Authentication tokens are stored locally on your device.

5. Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

To request account deletion:

  1. Send an email to shvietsovroman@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
  2. Include the email address associated with your Google account.

Upon receiving your request, we will permanently delete your account and all associated data (body metrics, meal history, nutrition data) within 30 days. Google account credentials are managed by Google and are not deleted by us.
