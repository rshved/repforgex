Support

App: RepForgeX
Developer: Roman Shvietsov
Contact: shvietsovroman@gmail.com

RepForgeX is a training and nutrition tracker: log your workouts, build and share training programs, and track calories and macros — including by photographing a meal and having AI estimate it.

Getting Help

Email shvietsovroman@gmail.com with a description of the problem. We normally reply within 2 business days. To help us reproduce the issue, please include your device model, iOS version, and the email address you signed in with.

AI Credits

AI meal analysis is paid for with AI credits: 1 credit per text entry and 2 credits per photo. Everything else in the app — workouts, programs, the library, and manual meal logging — is free.

Credits are purchased in packs through the App Store and are added to your balance immediately after the purchase is confirmed. If a purchase was charged but the credits did not appear, open the app once while online — the purchase is re-verified automatically on launch. If the balance is still wrong, email us with the date and amount of the purchase.

Refunds

Purchases made through the App Store are refunded by Apple, not by us. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. Once Apple approves a refund, the corresponding credits are removed from your balance.

Account Deletion

Open Profile, scroll to the bottom, and tap Delete account. Your account and all associated data — body metrics, meal history, training programs, session history, and any remaining AI credits — are deleted immediately and permanently. Deletion cannot be undone, and remaining AI credits are forfeited.

If you no longer have access to the app, email us with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and we will delete your account within 30 days.

Reporting Content

Programs published to the library are created by other users. If you see something inappropriate, open the program and use Report, or block the author so their programs no longer appear for you. Reported content is reviewed and removed if it breaks the rules, normally within 24 hours.
